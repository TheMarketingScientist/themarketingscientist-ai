Advisory Services

Marketing Performance & Economics

Understand how marketing creates profitable growth, what is constraining performance and what the company should change.

This service connects five parts of the marketing value system:

  • Executive outcomes: revenue growth, contribution margin, cash generation, customer-base growth and capital efficiency.
  • Customer economics: acquisition cost, lifetime value, payback, retention, repeat purchasing, cohort quality and customer profitability.
  • Commercial drivers: pricing, promotions, penetration, share of wallet, product and customer mix, distribution, cannibalization and cost to serve.
  • Marketing drivers: brand, media allocation, reach, frequency, creative, audiences, conversion, search, promotional execution and availability.
  • Measurement and evidence: experiments, Marketing Mix Modeling, incrementality, cohort analysis, causal inference, forecasting and scenario analysis.

Evidence levels

Evidence-Informed Diagnostic

Business data, established research, structured calculations, benchmarks and senior expert judgment.

Directional Analytics

Customer and commercial decompositions, cohort analysis, scenarios, lightweight econometrics and directional evidence.

Causal Measurement Program

Formal experiments, MMM, causal modeling, calibration, optimization, uncertainty analysis and measurement governance.

Typical questions

  • Is marketing producing profitable rather than merely attributable growth?
  • Which customer, commercial and marketing drivers explain current performance?
  • Are acquisition costs justified by customer quality, margin and payback?
  • Is the business overdependent on paid demand capture or discounting?
  • How should investment change across brand, media, pricing, promotions and customer growth?
  • What additional evidence is required before making a major budget decision?

Independent Investment Review

Determine whether a proposed investment is credible, economically justified and appropriate for the organization before committing budget, time and reputation.

Independent reviews may cover:

  • Agency proposals
  • Digital-transformation projects
  • Marketing technology
  • CRM and CDP initiatives
  • Artificial-intelligence initiatives
  • Analytics and measurement providers
  • Media strategies
  • Measurement and ROI claims
  • Commercial forecasts
  • Business cases

Each review considers

  • Credibility: Are the methodology, assumptions and promised outcomes defensible?
  • Economic justification: Are the costs, expected value, risks and payback realistic?
  • Organizational fit: Does the company have the data, capabilities, processes and adoption conditions required?
  • Alternatives: Is there a simpler, cheaper or more appropriate way to solve the problem?
  • Execution risk: What dependencies, incentives, hidden costs and implementation risks are being overlooked?

The output is an independent recommendation to approve, revise, negotiate, postpone or reject the proposed initiative.
