Advisory Services

Marketing Performance & Economics

Identify how marketing creates profitable growth and what is constraining profitability. Then act to make growth more profitable!

This service connects five parts of the marketing value system:

  • Financial outcomes: revenue growth, contribution margin, cash generation, customer base growth and capital efficiency.
  • Customer economics: acquisition cost, lifetime value, payback, retention, repeat purchasing, cohort quality and customer profitability.
  • Commercial drivers: pricing, promotions, penetration, share of wallet, product and customer mix, distribution, cannibalization and cost to serve.
  • Marketing drivers: brand, share of voice, media allocation, reach, frequency, creative, audiences, conversion, search, promotional execution and availability.
  • Measurement and evidence: experiments, Attribution Modeling, incrementality, cohort analysis, causal inference, forecasting and scenario analysis.

Evidence levels

Evidence-Informed Diagnostic

Business data, established research, structured calculations, benchmarks and senior expert judgment.

Directional Analytics

Customer and commercial decompositions, cohort analysis, directional econometrics and evidence.

Marketing Value Decision System

Formal experiments and attribution, causal modeling, calibration, optimization, uncertainty analysis and measurement governance.

Typical questions

  • Is marketing producing profitable or just attributable growth?
  • Which customer, commercial and marketing drivers explain current performance?
  • Are customer acquisition costs justified by customer quality, margin and payback?
  • Is the business overdependent on paid demand or discounting/promotions?
  • How should investment change across brand, media, pricing, promotions and customer growth?
  • What additional evidence is required before making a major budget decision?

Independent and Unbiased Investment Review

Determine whether a proposed investment is credible, economically justified and appropriate for the organization before committing budget, time and reputation.

Independent reviews may cover:

  • Agency Measurement proposals
  • Measurement Vendor Proposals and Comparisons
  • Data Sourcing, CRM and CDP initiatives
  • AI initiatives
  • Marketing Analytics and measurement providers
  • Vendor ROI claims and Budget Allocation Recommendations
  • Agency or Vendor Reliability for budget allocation and investment decision making
  • Business cases

Each review considers

  • Credibility: How biased and reliable are budget allocation recommendations, methodologies used, assumptions and promised outcomes.
  • Economic justification: Are the costs, expected value, risks and payback realistic?
  • Organizational fit: Does the company have the data, capabilities, processes and adoption conditions required?
  • Alternatives: Is there a simpler, cheaper or more appropriate way to solve the problem?
  • Execution risk: What dependencies, incentives, hidden costs and implementation risks are being overlooked?
